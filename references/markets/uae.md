# United Arab Emirates: starter market profile

Starting point only. **Verify every line and cite a current source before using it.** Numbers (population, penetration, platform shares) are deliberately left out because they change; research them fresh.

| Area | Starter note |
|---|---|
| Currency | UAE dirham (AED) |
| Weekend | Saturday-Sunday (since 2022; check private-sector practice) |
| Time zone | UTC+4 (Asia/Dubai) |
| Phone code | +971 |
| Languages | Arabic (official); English dominant in commerce; large South Asian audiences |
| Payments | Visa/Mastercard, Apple Pay, BNPL Tabby and Tamara; check the domestic card scheme status |
| Data protection | Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (PDPL); DIFC and ADGM have their own data laws |
| Key dates | Commemoration Day 30 November, National Day 2-3 December; Islamic holidays move each year. Ramadan and Eid follow the Hijri calendar and move about 11 days earlier each year. |
| Notes | Very international audience and heavy tourism: multi-language and tourist content matter. |
